Color Palette & Hex Codes

1. Dominant / Primary Brand Colors
   Deep Navy / Slate Blue (#1A2A3A / #0E1B29)

Role: Primary typography, hero section backgrounds, header elements, and dark container boxes.

Vibe: Establishes trust, authority, professionalism, and security—standard for healthcare and Medicare brokerages.

Clean White / Soft Off-White (#FFFFFF / #F8F9FA)

Role: Page background, card backgrounds, and contrast text on dark elements.

Vibe: Creates an open, readable layout with generous whitespace to ensure healthcare choices feel clear and uncluttered.

2. Secondary / Accent Colors
   Vibrant Medical Blue / Teal Accent (#007AFF / #00A8B5)

Role: Interactive call-to-action (CTA) buttons (e.g., "Contact Us", "Become an Agent"), active links, and key highlights.

Vibe: Provides modern contrast against dark navy and clean white, guiding the user’s eye toward action steps.

Soft Slate / Muted Charcoal Gray (#4A5568 / #6B7280)

Role: Body text, secondary subheadings, disclaimers, and form labels.

Vibe: Lowers visual strain compared to stark black text, keeping reading comfortable and modern.
